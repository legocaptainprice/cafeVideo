# Community Guidelines

These guidelines will lay out what is and what is not allowed on cafeVideo. This includes, but is not limited to comments, uploads, profiles and all other forms of interactions on cafeVideo.

## Respect other users and content creators

Treat all users and content creators with respect and solve non-platform related issues outside of cafeVideo. Any threats, harassment and doxxing will result in disciplinary action.

## Illegal / NSFW Content

All forms of illegal content are not allowed on cafeVideo, this includes, but is not limited to:

### Illegal

- Pirated / illegally obtained video and audio content.
- Content that contains real-life violence, gore and harm.
- Content that contains footage of content that was illegally obtained.
- Content that is filming areas, people and legal authority in non-public spaces without express permission.

This section covers NSFW content, from what is allowed to what isn't allowed:

### NSFW Content

- Content that is simulated violence, gore and sexual content is allowed on cafeVideo, examples include: video games, movies and other forms of fictional media.
- Content that contains real-life violence, gore, harm and sexual content is not allowed on cafeVideo.
- Content that contains AI-Generated content that aims to depict real-life politicians, creators, and other people in a harmful, misleading or sexual context is not allowed on cafeVideo.
- Content that involves inappropriate language that is not harmful or hatred, is allowed on cafeVideo.

## Misuse of Platform

Do not use cafeVideo to:

- Run, promote or encourage, scams, targeted harassment campaigns, spam content, malicious content and any disallowed content.
- Manipulate, exploit or hack the systems, bugs and glitches on cafeVideo.
- Falsely report creators or users on the platform.
- Conduct illegal activity.
